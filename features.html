Features

Everything you need, and it works offline.

Courses hold study sets, and each set comes with its own notes, quiz and flashcard deck. On top of that there's a planner, a focus timer and a bit of XP to keep you going.

Notes

Notes that actually stick.

A block editor built for lecture notes. You can also type Markdown-style shortcuts.

  • Headings, paragraphs, bullet and numbered lists, quotes and dividers
  • Tables that scroll sideways on a phone
  • LaTeX maths and code blocks
  • Callouts, including exam bait for the things your lecturer keeps hinting at
  • Images from your gallery or files
  • Bold, italic, underline, highlight, inline code and links

Quizzes

Quizzes that grade you.

Build a quiz from parts, mixing the question types however you like.

  • Multiple choice
  • Identification, with more than one accepted answer
  • Enumeration and ordered enumeration, with partial credit
  • Matrix: fill the grid from a word bank
  • Fill in the blanks
  • A review screen after every attempt, plus XP for each right answer

Flashcards

Review right before you forget.

Every card is scheduled with the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm.

  • Rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy
  • See when the card comes back before you pick
  • A daily review queue across all your courses
  • Import Anki .apkg decks, or export your cards as .apkg or CSV

Plan

Exam in 5 days? Sorted.

A planner for classes, study blocks and exams, with a cram plan when time is short.

  • Import your class timetable from an .ics file
  • Tick what the exam covers and choose how long you can study each day
  • Weakest topics first, in exam order or spaced out
  • Exam mode takes over the home screen as the date gets close
  • Optional reminders, even when the app is closed

Keep going

Keep the streak alive.

Small rewards that make it easier to open the app again tomorrow.

  • XP for answers, reviews and focus time, and levels that are never lost
  • A daily goal and a streak, with freezes to bank for busy days
  • Outfits for the mascot and badges to unlock
  • A focus timer with rain, café, lo-fi and brown-noise sounds, all built in
  • Optional sound effects and haptics

Your files, your call

Getting things in and out.

Backups

Save your whole library to one .noggin file and keep it wherever you like. Restoring replaces what's on the device, so Noggin makes you type RESTORE first.

Share a set

Export one set, a course or several courses as a .noggin file. A friend opens it and picks what to import.

Import from text

Noggin gives you instructions to paste into the AI chat you already use. Paste its answer back in and Noggin builds the notes, quiz and cards, and marks any line it couldn't read. Noggin itself never connects to the AI.

Where it runs

Phone, tablet and PC.

Android

Android 7.0 or later, on phones and tablets in both orientations. Coming soon to Google Play.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installer doesn't need admin rights, and you can open .noggin files by double-clicking them.
