What's new

    One set, three ways to learn

    Write it once. Study it three ways.

    Every study set comes with its own notes, quiz and flashcard deck, all kept together.

    Notes that stick

    A block editor with headings, lists, tables, LaTeX maths, code, images and exam-bait callouts.

    Quizzes that grade you

    Six question types: multiple choice, identification, enumeration, ordered lists, matrix and fill in the blanks. Partial answers get partial credit.

    Cards that come back on time

    Spaced repetition schedules every card, so you review it just before you'd forget.

    The offline promise

    Nothing leaves your device.

    Noggin works with no connection at all. When you're online, the only thing it asks is whether a newer version is out. Your notes stay on your phone or PC, and you decide where the backups go.

    • No accountOpen it and start. There's nothing to sign up for.
    • No Wi-FiIt works the same on a train, in a lecture hall or on a plane.
    • No adsNo banners, no pop-ups and no in-app purchases.
    • No trackingNo analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing you create is ever sent anywhere.
