Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short:

  • Noggin doesn't collect, sell or share any of your data.
  • The only thing it asks online is whether a newer version is out, at most once a day.
  • Everything you create stays on your device.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that track you.

This policy covers the Noggin app for Android and Windows ("Noggin", "the app"), made by LandChit ("we", "us"). It also covers this website.

What we collect

Nothing. Noggin has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we never receive your notes, quizzes, flashcards, study history or any information about you or your device.

Checking for updates

When Noggin opens and the device is online, it asks at most once a day for the number of the latest version, so it can tell you when a new one is out. That request carries nothing about you, your device or what you study, and all that comes back is the details of the latest release. Like any web request, the server that answers it can see technical details such as your IP address; we don't receive or keep them. If you're offline, Noggin skips the check and works exactly the same.

What stays on your device

To work, Noggin saves the things you create in a database on your device:

  • courses, study sets, notes, quizzes, flashcards and images you add
  • quiz attempts, flashcard review history and focus sessions
  • your planner (classes, study blocks and exams)
  • progress such as XP, level, streak, badges and outfits, plus the display name you choose
  • your settings

On Android this data is in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. On Windows it's in %APPDATA%\dev.landchit\Noggin in your user profile. We can't see or reach any of it.

Files you choose to open or save

Noggin only reads or writes a file outside its own storage when you ask it to:

  • Backups and exports (.noggin), Anki decks (.apkg) and CSV files are saved where you pick. After that, what happens to them is up to you and the service you keep them in, such as a cloud drive or a chat app.
  • Imports: Noggin reads a .noggin, .apkg or .ics timetable file only when you open or pick it.
  • Images you add to notes are copied into Noggin's storage.

Import from text and AI tools

Import from text gives you instructions you can paste into an AI chat of your choice. Noggin doesn't connect to that AI or send it anything. Whatever you paste into another service is covered by that service's privacy policy, not this one. Noggin only reads the text you paste back in.

Noggin doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone state or advertising ID. Files and images are picked with the system file picker, so Noggin doesn't need access to all your storage.

Google Play data safety

In Google Play's terms: no data collected and no data shared with third parties. The update check sends no personal or study data and goes over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Children

Noggin collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and has no ads or social features.

Deleting your data

All of your data is on your device, so you're the one who controls and deletes it. There's no account, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

  • Delete individual courses, sets, notes, cards or quizzes in the app.
  • Android: go to Settings → Apps → Noggin → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Either one removes everything.
  • Windows: uninstall Noggin from Settings → Apps and answer Yes when it asks whether to delete your Noggin data. Or delete the %APPDATA%\dev.landchit\Noggin folder yourself.
  • Backups and exports you saved elsewhere stay where you put them. Delete those files as you would any other file.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses to run the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. On the download buttons, your browser asks GitHub for details of the latest Windows release.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If Noggin ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here before that version ships.
